Analysis
  Client p50 is ~162 ms at every level; server-side warm handler time (M2) is
  69.6 ms, so roughly 90 ms is network and API Gateway. Latency does not rise
  with load, and ok/s matches Little's-law N/R throughout: no queueing.

  Why the expected throttling above 10 clients did not appear: Lambda
  concurrency is (arrival rate x server time), not client count. At 20 clients
  that is 101.8 req/s x 0.0696 s = 7.1 concurrent executions, under the
  10-execution lab cap. Clients spend most of each cycle waiting on the
  network, not occupying a Lambda. The test therefore did NOT reach the
  system's ceiling; it shows the system is not the bottleneck at 101.8 req/s.

  Predicted ceiling (untested): 10 / 0.0696 s = ~144 req/s for ingest alone,
  lower if the threshold Lambda's invocations draw on the same cap.
  In users at one reading a minute: measured 101.8 req/s = ~6,100 users;
  predicted ceiling ~144 req/s = ~8,600 users.
